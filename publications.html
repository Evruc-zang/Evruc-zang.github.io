  1. Genome-Wide Association Study of Yield-Related Traits and Photoperiod Response in Rice

    Zang, Z.; Liu, C.; Wang, Z.; Fan, C.; Chen, J.*   First author 第一作者

    Plants, 2026, 15(6): 875. MDPI.

    • JCR Q1
    • IF 4.7
    • ResearchGate
    Abs DOI PDF

    Applied statistical genetic models to genome-wide association studies (GWAS) and photoperiod response analysis for yield-related traits in rice, including functional enrichment and candidate gene discovery, enabling the mining of elite germplasm carrying pleiotropic genes in rice.

    基于统计遗传模型对水稻产量相关性状开展全基因组关联分析（GWAS）与光周期响应分析， 完成功能富集与候选基因寻找，实现水稻一因多效基因的优良种质挖掘。
