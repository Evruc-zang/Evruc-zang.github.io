Multi-modal Intelligent Monitoring of the Full Wheat Phenology

小麦全物候期多模态智能监测

2025.05 – 2026.05

National Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program · Second Principal Investigator · Supervisor: Jin Shichao

国家级大学生创新创业训练计划项目 · 第二负责人 · 导师：金时超

  • Algorithm design: Built an image visual-feature extractor on a Transformer architecture, modelled environmental information with sequence models such as LSTM, fused multi-modal features through MLPs, and designed a plot segmentation algorithm to achieve accurate, intelligent recognition of wheat phenological stages.
  • Core work: Constructed a full-phenology monitoring dataset for wheat from UAV RGB imagery and contributed to a multi-modal data-fusion algorithm that integrates environmental information.
  • Outcome: The project passed its national-level completion review, delivering the wheat phenology dataset and the multi-modal fusion algorithm.
  • 算法搭建：基于 Transformer 架构构建图像视觉特征提取器，使用 LSTM 等序列模型建模环境信息，基于 MLP 等融合多模态特征，并设计小区分割算法，实现小麦物候期的精准智能识别。
  • 核心工作：基于无人机 RGB 图像构建小麦全物候期监测数据集，参与开发融合环境信息的多模态数据融合算法。
  • 项目成果：项目以国家级结项验收，完成小麦全物候期监测数据集构建与多模态数据融合算法开发。
  • Transformer
  • LSTM
  • MLP FusionMLP 融合
  • UAV RGB无人机 RGB
  • Phenology Recognition物候期识别

GWAS of Yield-Related Traits in Rice and Identification of Small-Effect Polygenes

水稻产量性状 GWAS 与微效多基因识别

2025.09 – 2026.02

Research Assistant · Supervisor: Zhang Jin

科研助理 · 导师：张瑾

  • Algorithm design: Using compressed variance-component mixed models, brought additive and dominance effects together with their polygenic background into a unified Bayesian framework, and applied a two-stage strategy of genome-wide scanning plus machine learning to identify small-effect polygenes accurately from sparse solutions.
  • Core work: Applied statistical genetic models to genome-wide association studies (GWAS), functional enrichment and candidate gene discovery for yield-related traits in rice.
  • Outcome: Enabled the mining of elite germplasm carrying pleiotropic genes in rice; the results were applied in a 2026 article in Plants (JCR Q1, IF 4.7).
  • 算法搭建：通过压缩方差组分混合模型，将加性、显性效应及其多基因背景纳入统一的 Bayesian 框架，并利用全基因组扫描 + 机器学习的两阶段策略，从稀疏解中精准识别微效多基因。
  • 核心工作：使用统计遗传模型对水稻产量性状进行全基因组关联分析（GWAS）、功能富集与候选基因寻找。
  • 项目成果：完成水稻一因多效基因的优良种质挖掘，研究成果已应用于 Plants（JCR Q1, IF 4.7）2026 年论文。
  • Bayesian FrameworkBayesian 框架
  • GWAS
  • Variance-Component Model方差组分模型
  • Candidate Genes候选基因

2026.02 · Singapore 2026.02 · 新加坡

NTU winter school “AI & GPT” · Visiting student · Project lead

南洋理工大学冬校《AI & GPT》 · 访问学生 · 项目主持
