Research Assistant

科研助理

2025.09 – 2026.02

Supervisor: Zhang Jin

导师：张瑾

  • Algorithm design: Using compressed variance-component mixed models, brought additive and dominance effects together with their polygenic background into a unified Bayesian framework, and applied a two-stage strategy of genome-wide scanning plus machine learning to identify small-effect polygenes accurately from sparse solutions.
  • Core work: Applied statistical genetic models to genome-wide association studies (GWAS), functional enrichment and candidate gene discovery for yield-related traits in rice.
  • Outcome: Enabled the mining of elite germplasm carrying pleiotropic genes in rice; the results were applied in Zang, Z.; Liu, C.; Wang, Z.; Fan, C.; Chen, J.* Genome-Wide Association Study of Yield-Related Traits and Photoperiod Response in Rice. [J] Plants. 2026; 15(6): 875. (JCR Q1, IF 4.7)
  • 算法搭建：通过压缩方差组分混合模型，将加性、显性效应及其多基因背景纳入一个统一的 Bayesian 框架，并利用全基因组扫描 + 机器学习的两阶段策略，实现了从稀疏解中精准识别微效多基因。
  • 核心工作：使用统计遗传模型对水稻产量性状进行全基因组关联分析（GWAS）、功能富集与候选基因寻找。
  • 项目成果：实现了对水稻一因多效基因的优良种质挖掘，研究成果已应用于 Zang, Z.; Liu, C.; Wang, Z.; Fan, C.; Chen, J.* Genome-Wide Association Study of Yield-Related Traits and Photoperiod Response in Rice. [J] Plants. 2026; 15(6): 875.（JCR Q1, IF 4.7）
